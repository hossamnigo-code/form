Our Story

Three decades rooted in Egyptian soil

Agroland began in 1991 as a single station with one production line, preparing just 50 tons of citrus and potatoes a day. From that small beginning, El-Naggar Group built one of Egypt's most respected names in fresh-produce preparation, packing and export.

Since our major expansion in 2004, upgraded lines now handle up to 500 tons of citrus, 300 tons of potatoes and 50 tons of onions every day — all under one integrated, traceable quality system, from the field to the final container.
